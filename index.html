What it does

Hushette lives in the menu bar. Click it, pick the sounds you like and set each one's level: white, pink or brown noise, rain, surf, wind, a fan, a low drone.

  • Never a loop. Every sound is made as it plays, and drifts slowly: rain grows heavier and lighter, waves come in sets, wind gusts. There is no seam to catch your ear, and the app is tiny.
  • Quiet for your calls. When a call starts, Hushette fades out, and fades back in when it ends.
  • A mix for every part of the day. Save mixes such as Work, Reading or Sleep, and let Hushette switch between them by the clock.
  • Tuned to your room. Let Hushette listen for a few seconds and it shapes a noise to cover the hum of your fridge, traffic or office. Nothing it hears is recorded or kept.
  • A sleep timer that fades out gently.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Privacy in one paragraph

Hushette has no network access, no account and no tracking. It notices only whether a microphone is in use, so it can pause for calls, and it uses the microphone itself only when you ask it to tune to your room, for a few seconds, without keeping anything. The full privacy policy says the same at more length.
