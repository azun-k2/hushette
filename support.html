Common questions

Why did the sound stop by itself?

Either a call started (Hushette pauses while any app uses a microphone), or the sleep timer ran out. The panel says which. You can turn call pausing off in Settings.

What does "Tune to my room" do?

It listens through your microphone for a few seconds, finds which pitches are loudest around you, and makes a noise shaped to cover them. macOS asks for microphone access the first time. The sound it hears is analysed on your Mac and thrown away at once.

How do presets and the schedule work?

A preset is a saved mix. With the schedule on, Hushette switches to the preset you chose for each part of the day while it is playing.

Does it use much battery?

Very little. The sounds are generated efficiently, and nothing runs while Hushette is paused.
