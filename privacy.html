The short version

Hushette collects nothing, sends nothing and has no network access.

What Hushette notices

Whether a microphone is in use by any app, so it can pause during calls. It reads a single on or off flag from macOS. This needs no microphone permission and hears nothing.

Your microphone, only when you ask. If you press Tune to my room, macOS asks for microphone access, and Hushette listens for a few seconds to measure how loud each band of pitches is. The sound is analysed in memory and discarded at once; it is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Only the resulting shape of the noise is kept.

What is stored, and where

Inside the app's own sandbox container on your Mac: your mixes, presets, schedule and settings. Deleting the app removes them.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Hushette has no network entitlement, which means macOS itself will not let it open a network connection. There is no server, no analytics and no account.
